These terms govern the career coaching provided by HIO Group, Inc. (brand name "Glow Booking Studio," the "Service"). By booking and paying for a session, you agree to these terms.

1. What the Service is

The Service is non-clinical coaching to help you sort through career direction, work-related decisions, priorities, and your options ahead. It does not provide psychotherapy, mental health diagnosis or treatment, medical care, crisis intervention, legal or immigration advice, or job placement.

If a matter falls outside the scope of coaching, the Service may decline to proceed and, where appropriate, suggest more suitable support.

2. Eligibility and service area

The Service is for adults aged 18 and over. Where the Service can be offered is determined by applicable law and professional regulation, and your location may be confirmed before booking.

3. Booking, pricing, and payment

  • A free 10-minute intro call is available.
  • The 45-minute paid session is offered at an introductory price of $100. This applies to the first five confirmed paid bookings, or through January 31, 2027, whichever comes first. The regular price thereafter is $150.
  • The price that applies to you is confirmed before your booking and payment are finalized.
  • Payment is private pay only; health insurance is not accepted.
  • A paid session is confirmed once you have reviewed the price, payment method, time, and scope, and the required consent and payment are complete.
  • There are no ongoing contracts or automatic renewals.

4. Cancellation, rescheduling, and refunds

  • The free 10-minute intro call may be cancelled anytime at no cost.
  • If you give notice at least 24 hours before a paid session, you may reschedule for free or cancel for a full refund.
  • For changes within 24 hours of the start time, one complimentary reschedule is offered, provided you give notice before the start time.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours, and no-shows without prior notice, are generally non-refundable — except where a refund is required by applicable law or where the Service allows an exception.
  • If the Service cannot hold a session for its own reasons, you may choose a reschedule or a full refund.
  • If a session cannot take place due to a technical or connection problem, the Service will review the situation and arrange a re-session, reschedule, or appropriate refund.
  • If, before the session begins, the Service determines the matter is outside the scope of coaching, the session will not be held and any payment made is refunded in full.

5. Limits and outcomes

The Service does not guarantee any particular employment result, decision, increase in income, or improvement in psychological symptoms. Important professional decisions and actions are yours to make.

6. Emergencies

The Service is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services (911 in the U.S.). For a mental health crisis in the U.S., you can call or text 988.

7. Personal data and confidentiality

Personal data is handled under the separate Privacy Policy. Confidentiality has legal exceptions and is not absolute.

8. Session records and recording

The Service may keep records of booking, consent, payment, and sessions as needed. As a rule, it does not record, film, or auto-transcribe sessions. If an exception is made, you will be told in advance and the required consent obtained.

9. Declining or stopping a session

The Service may decline to hold a session if the matter is beyond its scope, where there is a reason under applicable law, or if it cannot be delivered safely and appropriately. In that case, how any payment is treated is decided based on the circumstances and applicable law.

10. Liability

The Service provides coaching with reasonable care but does not guarantee specific outcomes. Liability is subject to applicable law and the specific terms of your engagement. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

11. Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the update date will be shown. For bookings confirmed before a change, material terms will not be applied retroactively against you.

12. Governing law and contact

Except for applicable mandatory law, these terms are governed by the laws of the State of New York, United States. For questions, complaints, or cancellation and refund requests, email glowbookingstudio@gmail.com.
