Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Glow Booking Studio ("the Service") provides career clarity coaching. This policy explains how information is handled when you use this website (glowbookingstudio.com) and the Service.

Information collected

  • The website itself does not collect personal information or use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • When you book an intro call or session, or email us, we receive the information you provide — such as your name, email address, and preferred times.
  • What you choose to share during a session.

How information is used

Information is used only to schedule and provide your call or session and to respond to your inquiries. It is never sold or shared with third parties without your consent.

Third-party services

The Service relies on the following providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Scheduling tool (Calendly): processes your name, email, and appointment details.
  • Email (Google): used for correspondence and inquiries.
  • A video-call tool may be used for online sessions.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses no analytics or advertising cookies. Note that loading the web fonts used for display (Google Fonts) may send your IP address to the provider.

Confidentiality

What you share in a session is treated with care and is not disclosed to others, except where required by law or where necessary to protect the safety of you or another person.

This is a non-clinical coaching service, not psychotherapy or a medical service. It is not subject to clinical confidentiality rules or legal protections such as HIPAA.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. To review or delete your information, email the address below.

Minors

The Service is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

International data

The Service is based in the United States. If you use it from Japan or elsewhere, your information may be processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email glowbookingstudio@gmail.com.
