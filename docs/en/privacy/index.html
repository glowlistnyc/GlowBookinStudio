Privacy Policy

HIO Group, Inc. (brand name "Glow Booking Studio," the "Service") provides online, non-clinical career coaching. This policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, and disclosed in connection with this website and the Service.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following information as needed:

  • Your name, email address, and preferred language
  • Appointment dates and status, and reschedule/cancellation information
  • State or country information used to confirm where the Service can be offered
  • The topics or questions you choose to share
  • Records of paid bookings, payments, and refunds
  • Session records created to the extent needed to deliver the Service
  • Records of your agreement to the consent form and the terms

We do not ask for sensitive information such as medical details, diagnosis history, or immigration status as part of ordinary booking.

2. How information is used

Information is used to manage bookings, respond to inquiries, confirm whether the Service is a fit, deliver sessions, manage payments and refunds, keep records, meet legal obligations, and respond where necessary for safety. We do not use it for marketing emails or unrelated marketing without your explicit consent.

3. Third-party providers

The Service uses outside providers as needed to operate. These providers may process information in the course of their services, and each is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Scheduling: Calendly
  • Email correspondence: Google
  • Website hosting and delivery: a hosting provider
  • Online sessions: a video-call service (to be named once chosen)
  • Payments and refunds: a payment service (to be named once chosen)
  • Record storage: an access-controlled storage service (to be named once chosen)

We do not sell your personal information. Sharing with these providers is limited to what is needed to perform the tasks above. We may disclose information where required by law, where necessary to protect legal rights, or to address a serious safety concern, in accordance with applicable law.

4. Website technical information

This website currently has no analytics or advertising tracking code of its own. However, loading the site may cause outside services such as the hosting provider and Google Fonts to process your IP address and technical connection information. If analytics or advertising tools are added in the future, this policy and the consent process will be revisited.

5. Session content and records

This is a non-clinical career coaching service. We may keep minimal records of the topics discussed, options considered, next actions, and any referrals offered.

As a rule, we do not record, film, or auto-transcribe sessions. If we ever do, we will explain the purpose and use in advance and obtain the required consent.

6. Confidentiality

Information you provide is handled with care and is not disclosed to third parties without a legitimate business need. However, disclosure may be required or permitted by law in cases such as legal obligations, lawful requests, or situations involving the safety of you or others.

This is not psychotherapy or a medical service. We handle information in accordance with the confidentiality and data-protection obligations that apply.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information with access controls, appropriate account authentication, storage practices, and backups.

8. Retention and deletion

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet legal retention, accounting, and dispute-handling needs. Specific retention periods are set based on applicable law and actual practice.

To access, correct, or delete your information, email us. In some cases we may be unable to delete information where retention is required by law.

9. Minors and international processing

The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The Service is based in the United States, and information may be processed in the United States or in the location of an outside provider.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date on this page will be revised. We will give separate notice of material changes where appropriate.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email glowbookingstudio@gmail.com.
