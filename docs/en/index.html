Coaching, counseling, and therapy

This service is non-clinical coaching focused on work-related decisions and the direction ahead. We sort through your situation, put your options into words, and find a clearer way forward.

Counseling and therapy are support from licensed professionals who work with mental health, symptoms, and emotional healing. Coaching is not a substitute, and it does not involve diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, or immigration or legal advice.

If low mood or anxiety is affecting your daily life, please reach out to a medical or mental health professional. When it helps, coaching can run alongside that support.

Frequently asked

Before you begin.

Is this therapy or counseling?

No. This is non-clinical career coaching, not psychotherapy, counseling, or mental health treatment. It does not involve diagnosis, treatment, or crisis intervention. If you need mental health support, I'll point you toward a medical or mental health professional.

How many sessions do I need?

It's designed as a single, self-contained 45-minute session. If you'd like to continue, you're welcome to book again whenever you want — there's no set number and no obligation to continue.

Do you review resumes or provide job placement?

No. This service focuses on clarifying career decisions and finding a clearer direction. It does not include resume reviews, job referrals, recruiting, or placement.

Can I book in Japanese or English?

Either is fine. Let me know your preferred language when you book, and we'll hold the session in Japanese or English.

What are LMSW and LSW?

They're U.S. social work credentials — LMSW in New York and LSW in New Jersey. In this service, I work as a non-clinical career coach, not in a clinical role, and I do not provide diagnosis or treatment.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can reschedule or cancel using the link in your booking confirmation email.
