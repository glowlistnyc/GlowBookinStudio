Coaching and therapy

Career clarity coaching is a non-clinical service focused on work-related decisions and the concrete actions ahead. We sort through your situation together, put your options into words, and find a manageable next step.

Therapy, by contrast, is clinical care provided by licensed professionals who work with mental health, symptoms, and emotional healing. Coaching is not a substitute for it, and it does not involve diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, or legal advice.

If low mood or anxiety is affecting your daily life, please reach out to a medical or mental health professional. When it helps, coaching can run alongside that support.
